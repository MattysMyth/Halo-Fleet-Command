Where things are

  • src/sim/combat.ts the pure battle simulator
  • src/sim/fleet.ts drydock, groups, sorties, repairs, offline Patrol
  • src/sim/marines.ts squads, missions, tactics, casualties, positions
  • src/sim/research.ts, modifiers.ts research projects, doctrines and the bonuses they fold into
  • src/sim/world.ts economy, fleet and marines stepped together
  • src/data/*.json every number the game uses
  • src/ui/fleet.tsx the Fleet screen: battle view, drydock, sectors
  • src/ui/operations.tsx the Operations screen: squads, mission view, positions
  • src/ui/research.tsx the Research screen; src/data/research.json its content
  • tools/combat-probe.ts win-rate table for sample fleets against each node
  • tools/odds-probe.ts squad odds by loadout against each position
  • tools/e2e/ browser harness with sample saves
